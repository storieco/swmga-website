About us

Serving Georgia since 1999.

Suburban Whitetail Management of North Georgia, Inc. is a Georgia nonprofit corporation. Our seal shows the mission: wildlife on one side, homes and families on the other, held in balance by the bow. Since 1999 we have worked in partnership with Georgia communities and the Georgia Department of Natural Resources, deploying highly qualified bowhunters to solve deer problems for property owners.
